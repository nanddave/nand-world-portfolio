        How this was built

        • Procedural three.js. Every building, car and kite is built from code: boxes, lathes and extrusions. No downloaded models.
        • A real Earth. Coastlines come from Natural Earth's public-domain land data, packed into a 32 KB bit mask. Mountains, biomes and clouds are generated.
        • Demos run in your browser. The missions do their work on your device; "Show your work" lists each step they actually ran. Anything scripted says so.
        • The co-pilot is search, not a chatbot. It ranks every story on the trip with BM25 plus a small synonym list, and shows you the match and its score.
        • Drive mode and real streets. The yellow car is arcade physics against box colliders derived from the scene; Bangalore's streets and buildings are OpenStreetMap data baked offline into a small JSON file (© OpenStreetMap contributors), with approximate building heights.
        • No paid services. No APIs, no tracking, no paid assets. Sound is synthesised live with WebAudio. It's a static site on free hosting.
